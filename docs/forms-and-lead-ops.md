# Forms and Lead Ops

## Forms added

- `wholesale-enquiry` posts to `/thank-you/` and includes a honeypot.
- `contact` posts to `/thank-you/` and includes a honeypot.

## Minimum lead fields

- Name / firm name
- Phone / WhatsApp
- Business type
- District
- SKU or pack size
- Monthly requirement
- GSTIN / FSSAI for trade and institutional buyers

## Lead stages

1. New enquiry
2. Called
3. Qualified
4. Sample sent
5. Rate card shared
6. Converted
7. Rejected / unqualified
