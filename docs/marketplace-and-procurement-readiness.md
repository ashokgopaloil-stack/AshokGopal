# Marketplace and Procurement Readiness

The site now has a `/procurement/` cluster for document-heavy buyers and channel-readiness work.

Use this cluster for:

- GeM-style procurement readiness
- Institutional buyer document checklists
- Bulk order checklists
- Restaurant rate-card preparation
- Retailer onboarding documents
- Distributor qualification scorecards
- Private-label qualification
- Marketplace listing data

Do not publish exact commercial rates until the business approves them. Keep pages as lead-capture and readiness pages until pack specs, MRP, carton count, and documents are final.
