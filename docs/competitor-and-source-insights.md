# Competitor and Source Insights

## Anveshan product page

Strong D2C product pages use many media slots: lab report image, customer review, comparison card, pungency card, smoke point card, nutrition card, seed sourcing card and pack variants.

**Apply:** Ashok Gopal needs a product-media gallery and proof slots, not one hero image.

## Saloni Oil homepage

Large mustard-oil brands emphasize operations, sourcing, quality control, packaging, R&D, capacity, certifications and offices.

**Apply:** Ashok Gopal should build manufacturing proof pages and not compete only on emotional brand copy.

## Ajanta Soya homepage

Industrial edible-oil sites expose company profile, brochure, manufacturing facility, distribution network, join distribution network, clientele, product catalogue, quality control, vendor registration and sales enquiry.

**Apply:** Add B2B assets, distributor qualification and procurement pages.

## Flipkart mustard oil category

Marketplace category pages organize demand around pack size, price comparison, brand options and related grocery searches, but often overuse broad health claims.

**Apply:** Use pack-size and marketplace readiness pages, but keep health claims conservative.

## GeM category page

Government procurement flows focus on buyer/seller registration, bid workflows, product categories and documentation.

**Apply:** Institutional pages need compliance data, rate-card readiness and procurement documents.

## Wikipedia mustard oil

Mustard oil is associated with Bengali/North Indian/South Asian cooking; expressed mustard oil is restricted for cooking in some North American/European countries due to erucic acid concerns.

**Apply:** Create a responsible safety/claims cluster that avoids medical overclaiming.
