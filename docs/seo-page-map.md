# SEO Page Map

This site is structured as a manufacturer-led SEO and B2B acquisition funnel.

## Core commercial pages

- `/products/` — product catalog hub.
- `/products/[slug]/` — SKU-specific pages for household, retail, food-service, and trade intent.
- `/wholesale/` — primary B2B lead form.
- `/wholesale/distributor/` — distributor intent.
- `/wholesale/super-stockist/` — larger territory partner intent.
- `/wholesale/retailer/` — kirana and grocery-store intent.
- `/wholesale/restaurant-supply/` — restaurant and caterer intent.
- `/wholesale/institutional-buyers/` — procurement/institutional intent.
